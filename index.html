Help

Book an appointment

Tap + Book appointment on the Door Board or Calendar. Fill in the carrier, driver, date and time, and the door. Everything else is optional but helps later: a phone number, the load type (inbound or outbound), and the PO or reference number are what make the evidence packet strong. Save, and the appointment shows on the board for its day.

Check a truck in and out

When the truck hits the yard, tap Check in on its appointment — that stamps the arrival time and starts its clock. When it backs into the door, tap At door. When it pulls out, tap Check out — DockClock does the detention math on the spot and shows you the charge before you confirm. Wrong time? Edit the appointment and fix the timestamps; every screen and total updates from the same record.

Tell DockClock (voice entry)

Tap Tell DockClock at the top — one tap and it's listening (on devices that support voice). Say things like "check in Johnson door four", "start unload door two", "check out Johnson", or "book Ozark Freight tomorrow at 8 am door two driver Sam Reed inbound". You always see a plain-words confirmation before anything is saved. If it can't understand a sentence, your exact words are kept as a note in the Tell screen — nothing is ever dropped or guess-saved. Voice needs a device/browser with speech recognition; typing the same sentences works exactly the same.

The detention money clock

Every checked-in truck runs a clock from its check-in time. Your free grace period and hourly rate live in Settings (and a load or carrier can carry its own terms when a contract differs). The clock is green inside grace, amber in the last 30 minutes of grace, and red once money is accruing. At check-out, time past grace is rounded up to the next full hour and charged at the rate — the math is printed line by line on the evidence packet.

Print an evidence packet (bill-back proof)

On any finished load, tap Packet. You get a printable page with the appointment, every timestamp, the grace/rate rules that applied, and the charge math — the document that goes with the invoice when you bill a carrier or shipper back. In the Log you can also print a carrier week packet: all of one carrier's finished loads for a week on one packet. Printing a packet marks that load's detention as "captured" in the Numbers view; finished loads with a charge but no packet yet show as "not yet billed" so the money doesn't quietly leak.

Your data & backups

Your data lives on this device only — DockClock has no account and sends nothing anywhere. To move or protect it, use Backup (JSON) in Settings and keep the file somewhere safe; Restore backup brings it back. If the browser's site data is cleared, the records go with it, so back up on any week you can't afford to lose.

Before you start

DockClock — dock scheduling & detention tracking for one facility. Sold and supported by White Wolf Apps (whitewolfbackoffice@gmail.com).

What it does: keeps your appointment book, stamps driver check-in / at-door / check-out times, runs a detention clock using the grace period and rate you set, computes detention charges, and prints evidence packets you can attach to an invoice.

What it does not do: DockClock is an independent tool for your own dock. It does not connect to, log into, or send anything to any retailer, carrier, or third-party system, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any retailer or carrier. Detention charges are calculated from the times and rules entered in the app — whether a charge is billable under a given contract is between you and that carrier or shipper; the app's packet is a record of times, not legal advice.

Your data: everything you enter stays on this device in this browser. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Backups are files you export and keep yourself.

Voice entry: where your device offers speech recognition, DockClock can take dictation through your device's own speech service. Voice is optional; every field also types.

By tapping Agree you accept these terms.
